Do you know where you want to go with your career, but feel unsure how to get there? Perhaps you're considering self-employment and don't know what's involved.

I can help you clarify your direction, work out what you need to do, then start moving towards it with intention.

Depending on how many sessions you choose, I’ll design a coaching programme for your specific needs.

For example:

- In one session, we can work on a smaller specific goal and find a way forward.
- In three sessions, we can create a more detailed plan, discuss strategies, and set milestones.
- In six sessions, we can go much deeper into all aspects and maintain accountability over a longer period.

You can find [more information about my coaching practice](/coaching/) or [contact me with any questions](/contact/).

✅ Coaching is right for you if:

- You are pursuing a new career (including self-employment).
- You are struggling with time, focus, and prioritisation.
- You are ready to try a different approach.

❌ Coaching isn't right for you if:

- You want an academic job or promotion.
- You don’t know what you want to do.
- You need a job immediately.
