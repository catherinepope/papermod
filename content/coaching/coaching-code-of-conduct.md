My coaching style is based on the following beliefs:

- Everyone’s life and experience are different. I’ll always treat you as an individual and provide a bespoke service.
- With the right support and encouragement, anyone can make progress towards their goals. - Coaching is all about action. My role is to increase your self-awareness and help close the gap between where you are and where you want to be. I do this by asking questions, challenging you, and holding you accountable.


## Code of Conduct

At all times, I shall:

- Treat clients with respect and without judgement.
- Maintain strict confidentiality.
- Avoid any conflict of interest.
- Remain impartial.
- Act in the client’s best interest, including recommending a different coach or a healthcare professional, if appropriate.
