Academic writing is hard. You're full of ideas, but everything else is getting in the way. I act as your accountability partner, providing structure, proven techniques, and new approaches. Together we build a realistic writing habit that works for *you*.

I can help with:

- PhD theses.
- Academic and professional papers.
- Non-fiction books and monographs.
- Writing workflow.
- Tools such as Scrivener and Zotero.

Depending on how many sessions you choose, I'll design a coaching programme for your specific needs.

For example:

- In one session, we can work on a specific obstacle and find a way forward.
- In three sessions, we can create a plan, discuss strategies, and set milestones.
- In six sessions, we can go much deeper into all aspects of your writing and maintain accountability over a longer period.

You can find [more information about my coaching practice](/coaching/) or [contact me with any questions](/contact/).

✅ Coaching is right for you if:

- You want to write and you already have an idea.
- You need someone to hold you accountable.
- You are struggling with time, focus, and prioritisation.

❌ Coaching isn't right for you if:

- You don't know what to write.
- You have no time for writing.
- You need help with editing or proofreading your work.
